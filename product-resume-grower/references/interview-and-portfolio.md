# Interview and portfolio expansion

Use the same evidence cards selected for the resume. Never add a stronger result or ownership claim only for storytelling.

## Interview story

Expand each selected claim using:

1. conclusion and personal role;
2. business/user background;
3. problem and constraints;
4. personal decisions and why;
5. delivery and collaboration;
6. result or current stage;
7. limitation and next iteration.

Generate follow-ups on ownership, alternatives, metrics, failure modes, product–engineering boundaries, domain logic, and what the candidate would change. Reuse the per-bullet follow-up probes already produced by the interview follow-up defense test in `references/resume-writing.md`: the resume, portfolio, and interview must answer the same probes with the same evidence, never a stronger one.

When evidence lacks outcome metrics, prepare a direct answer that names the current delivery stage and uses scale, quality, closure, or validation evidence instead.

## Portfolio case

Use this order:

- project thesis and context;
- user/business problem;
- role and responsibility boundary;
- key product decisions;
- selected flow/state/rule artifacts;
- delivery and validation;
- outcome, limitation, and next step.

Show only anonymized, externally permitted material. Recreate simplified diagrams when necessary; do not expose internal customer data, proprietary rules, or raw confidential screenshots.

## Consistency audit

Compare resume, portfolio, and interview versions for:

- identical ownership;
- identical stage and metrics;
- compatible scope and chronology;
- explicit distinction between company work and personal demo;
- explicit distinction between AI assistance and personal product judgment.
