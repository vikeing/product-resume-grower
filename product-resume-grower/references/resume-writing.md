# Product internship resume writing

## Page and section rules

Default to one A4 page for internship and campus recruiting. Prefer this order for Chinese internet-product applications:

1. Basic information
2. Education
3. Relevant internship experience
4. Selected projects
5. Skills and certificates

Use a short summary only when 1–2 lines create a clear, evidence-backed thesis that the rest of the resume proves. Remove it when it repeats the target title or uses generic traits.

## Bullet budget

- Core internship: 3–4 bullets.
- Sole strongly relevant internship: up to 5 non-redundant bullets.
- Anchor project: 2–3 bullets.
- Supporting project: 1–2 bullets.
- One bullet, one claim; target 1–2 rendered lines.
- Put reflection, learning, detailed fields, full exception trees, and low-value task lists in portfolio or interview material, not the resume.

## Bullet roles

Select a balanced subset:

1. **Core problem/outcome**: problem or goal + core solution + result/delivery evidence.
2. **Product judgment**: key decision + flow/state/rule/exception design + value.
3. **Delivery**: artifacts + collaboration + verified delivery stage.
4. **Data/validation**: data source + method + finding/decision.
5. **Differentiation**: AI boundary, risk strategy, payment depth, or another JD-relevant signal.

## Chinese style

Default to `【能力或项目标签】+ 业务问题/目标 + 关键判断/行动 + 交付或证据`.

Use strong, accurate verbs such as 梳理、定义、设计、拆解、完善、联动、推动、验收、验证、迭代. Avoid opening every line with 负责、参与 or 协助; use them when ownership needs to be constrained.

## English style

Remove bracketed labels. Start with an accurate action verb, use compact active voice, and preserve ownership and stage. Do not translate Chinese sentence structure mechanically.

## Results without growth metrics

Use the strongest available evidence in this order:

1. verified business or user outcome;
2. measurable scope;
3. delivery milestone;
4. quality, closure, auditability, or exception coverage;
5. explainable product judgment.

Do not invent percentage improvements. Do not present intended value as observed impact.

## Compression

Combine small tasks only when they share one business objective and capability. Do not merge unrelated projects, different ownership levels, company work with personal projects, or facts whose causal relationship cannot be explained.

Keep evidence-rich domain terms only when they improve target-role fit. Remove jargon that does not prove a decision, delivery, or result.

## Final audit

Check:

- every claim maps to evidence;
- ownership verbs match the card;
- stage and metrics are confirmed;
- no confidential details leak;
- bullets cover distinct capabilities;
- the page is not a work log;
- the resume remains explainable under interview follow-up.
